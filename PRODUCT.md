# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three co-equal audiences, all Medellín/Colombia-based or destination clients:
- Engaged couples planning luxury weddings
- Corporate planners booking product launches, galas, and branding events
- Private clients booking milestone celebrations (birthdays, anniversaries, soirées)

All three arrive comparing event-design studios and deciding who to trust with a high-stakes, high-budget occasion. They're browsing on desktop and mobile, often at night, skimming for proof of taste and reliability before committing to a consultation. The job to be done: convince the visitor this studio has both artistic range and flawless execution, then get them to book a private consultation via the contact form.

## Product Purpose

Lottus Designers is a bilingual (EN/ES) luxury event design and planning studio based in Medellín, Colombia, serving weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations. The site exists to showcase the studio's portfolio and process, build immediate trust in its craftsmanship, and convert visitors into booked consultations. Success is a qualified lead submitted through the contact/questionnaire section.

## Brand Personality

Opulent, romantic, meticulous — with a stated desire to also feel **premium and modern**, not dated or overly ornate. Voice is warm but precise: confident white-glove language ("bespoke," "flawless," "white-glove"), never salesy or exclamatory. Visual personality should read as gallery-quality and cinematic (gold accent, champagne, charcoal, serif display type) rather than "cute" or "whimsical." Motion is part of the brand: animation should feel intentional and cinematic, reinforcing premium polish, not decorative flourish.

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid the generic wedding-planner-template look: Wix/Squarespace boilerplate layouts, cursive script fonts, stock-photo confetti/rose-petal imagery, pastel-heavy palettes, cluttered card grids, and anything that reads as a DIY invitation site rather than a professional design studio's own portfolio.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — let the portfolio, gallery, and cinematic imagery carry the "luxury" claim; copy should describe outcomes, not adjectives-stack.
2. **Cinematic restraint** — one strong visual/animation moment per section beats several competing ones; motion should feel directed, not default-template.
3. **Craft in the details** — gold-accent micro-interactions, spacing, and type hierarchy should read as designed by the same hand that designs the events.
4. **Bilingual parity** — English and Spanish content must feel equally native and complete; never a machine-translated afterthought.
5. **Frictionless path to consultation** — every section should make it obvious and easy to get to the contact form without pressure or clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline for contrast and interactive elements across the marketing site. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for scroll-triggered animations, the preloader, and the stats counters (crossfade/instant-state fallback instead of suppressing content). No motion-gated content that fails to render for reduced-motion or non-JS users.
